Privacy Policy

Privacy information for the Tastify restaurant showcase website.

Last updated: 6 October 2026

Important: This is a general website privacy notice prepared for a static restaurant showcase. Tastify should review it and adapt it to its actual practices before publishing it as a legal policy.

1. About this website

This website provides general information about Tastify Arabian Mandi & Restaurant, including restaurant imagery, menu references, contact details, and directions. It is not an online ordering, payment, account, or reservation system.

2. Information collected by this website

The static pages in this project do not include a contact form, account registration, online checkout, or a custom backend that stores visitor submissions. The website owner should update this policy if analytics, advertising, embedded services, forms, or other data-collection tools are added later.

3. Third-party services and links

Some content may load from third-party services, including publicly hosted restaurant images, Google Maps links, web fonts, and external restaurant listings. When your browser connects to those providers, they may receive technical information such as your IP address, browser details, or the page requested, and may use cookies or similar technologies according to their own policies. This website does not control those providers.

  • Google Maps: Google Privacy Policy
  • Google Fonts: fonts may be requested from Google servers.
  • Public listing images: images are hosted by the third-party publisher that supplies them.

4. How information is used

Information voluntarily shared by calling the restaurant or messaging through WhatsApp is handled through those separate communication services and by the recipient. This static showcase does not receive those messages through its own server.

5. Retention and security

This website project does not create its own visitor database. Third-party services may retain information according to their own terms and policies. No website can guarantee absolute security of internet communications.

6. Children

This website is intended as general restaurant information and is not designed to collect personal information from children.

7. Your choices

You can choose not to use external links or contact options. Browser settings may allow you to manage cookies and block third-party resources, although some visual features may not work as intended.

8. Contact

For restaurant-related privacy questions, contact Tastify at +91 99668 88060. The restaurant should add a dedicated privacy contact if one is established.

This page is a general website policy template. It should be reviewed by the restaurant owner and qualified counsel where appropriate before being relied upon as a legal document.
